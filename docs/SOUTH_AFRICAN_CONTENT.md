# South African catalogue refresh

The catalogue uses twelve South African artists: Kabza De Small, 25K, Tyla, A-Reece, DJ Maphorisa, Nasty C, Black Coffee, Ami Faku, Simmy, Sun-EL Musician, Young Stunna and K.O. Each profile contains two real catalogue song references. Artist background links are stored with each profile and exposed under Sources & image credits. YouTube links open searches for the relevant artist/song; they do not embed, host or license the recordings.

## Data provenance

Names and catalogue references are editorial selections. All scores, rankings, audiences, velocities, likes, shares and short-video uses are invented fixtures. They are not scraped social statistics, current charts, followers, monthly listeners, endorsements or verified performance claims. The seven-day and thirty-day controls still use the same demo snapshot. Search now includes song titles.

Background references consulted on 20 October 2026:

Other artist reference links are included in the fixtures for further editorial checking. No live social data was retrieved or integrated in this refresh.

All twelve artists also have original local SVG editorial artwork carrying their name and featured song. These are artist-specific graphics, not artist photographs, album covers or official promotional artwork. Artist-specific remote photo references are now configured for all twelve profiles. Spotify, Apple Music/Shazam, Concert Archives and Hï Ibiza references are stored per artist and credited in the profile. Their presence in public search does not establish a reuse license; these remain external reference images for the review prototype, with rights retained by their original owners. Approved production assets can replace them without changing profile data. A failed or slow remote photo falls back to that same artist's local graphic, with accurate alternative text. The profile retains the credit to the referenced photo.

Generic generated headphone/studio imagery remains atmospheric site art; it is not represented as a real artist portrait. FACTS SHOW concepts do not claim real artists have appeared as guests; its former artist earnings table is replaced by explicitly simulated song/fan activity.

Public imagery sources were found through image search on 20 October 2026. External availability and third-party hotlink behaviour can change; local editorial artwork is bundled with the app.

## Demo state

Commercial interactions remain simulations; no artist representation, booking availability, partnership or rights ownership is implied. Kabza is the sample identity in the active artist workspace. No real artist is contacted. The refresh uses a new browser storage key so the earlier fictional fixture state is preserved separately and does not get attributed to real artists. Previously saved prototype activity does not carry into this catalogue.
